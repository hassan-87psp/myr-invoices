Malaysia E-Invoice Guide: Prepare for MyInvois

Malaysia’s e-Invoice system involves structured transaction information and validation through HASiL's MyInvois system. Preparing complete business and customer records is a useful first step.

Ordinary invoice vs validated e-Invoice

An ordinary PDF or draft documents a transaction but does not, by itself, establish MyInvois validation. Check the official validation result before describing an e-Invoice as validated.

Prepare supplier and buyer information

Review your registered business name, taxpayer identification number, registration details, address and contact information. Which fields are required depends on the transaction and official guidance.

Prepare item details

Maintain clear descriptions, quantities, prices, tax treatment and the relevant classification codes. Review the current official code lists instead of guessing.

Check applicability and authorization

Check the latest HASiL implementation timeline and guidelines for your business. MYR Invoice is independent software; its live submission connection is pending activation. You can use it to organize invoices and prepare records meanwhile.
