MyInvois Preparation Checklist for Malaysian Businesses

Before configuring live e-Invoice submission, review the information your business uses to create sales documents. This checklist is for planning, not tax or legal advice.

1. Confirm current requirements

Read HASiL’s current timeline and specific guidelines. Check which requirements apply to your business and transaction type.

2. Check your company and customers

Verify registered names, taxpayer and registration identifiers, addresses and relevant contact details using appropriate official sources.

3. Check invoice items

Use clear item descriptions and review applicable tax, classification and unit codes. Verify invoice totals and adjustment references.

4. Test and review

Prepare a sample draft, preview the PDF and review validation checks. Live submission must wait for provider activation and authorization. Keep ordinary invoice and official validation statuses separate.

Official resources: HASiL e-Invoice guidelines · MyInvois SDK. Check the current official guidance for your business. MYR Invoice is independent software; live LHDN submission is pending activation.
